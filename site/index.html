EUC+ for Digitakt II

Melodies for the stock Euclidean sequencer, in the spirit of the Torso T-1. The Digitakt still decides the rhythm. EUC+ decides which note each hit plays: from a scale, along a phrase, with lockable randomness.

Experimental. EUC+ changes your instrument's firmware. It is not made, supported or endorsed by Elektron. Use it at your own risk, back up your projects first, and know how to recover your unit (below) before you flash anything.

How it works

Stock Euclidean mode (FUNC + AMP) makes the rhythm from two pulse generators: PL1, PL2, LEN, RO1, RO2, TRO and OP. EUC+ leaves all of that exactly as it is. Each time a Euclidean hit fires, EUC+ replaces the note the hit plays and leaves everything else in it alone.

1 · Stock EuclidWhere the hits fall: PL1, PL2, LEN, rotations, OP. The trig keys still light the hits in grid RECORD.
→
2 · EUC+Which note each hit plays: a note from the pool, moved by the phrase and the random sequence, kept in the scale.
→
3 · The hit playsWith the track's own sample or MIDI channel, velocity, filter, length and effects, as stock.
What it never does
It never adds, moves or removes a hit, and never writes pattern data. Switch EUC+ off and the next hit plays the stock note again. Your projects stay compatible with the stock OS.
Where it acts
Only on Euclidean hits, only on tracks where EUC+ is on, and only on steps without parameter locks: a locked step keeps the note you gave it. Audio tracks repitch the sample (NOTE 36–84). MIDI tracks send the note.

Using it

  1. On a track, press FUNC + AMP to open the stock Euclidean page, and switch EUC on as usual.
  2. Press FUNC + AMP again: the EUC+ pages open. Opening them changes nothing you can hear.
  3. Turn encoder H (ON) to switch EUC+ on for this track. The switch on the left of the screen fills and reads ON.
  4. Turn encoders A–H to shape the melody. While a knob turns, the title bar shows its name and value, as on stock pages.
  5. LEFT / RIGHT change page. NO or FUNC + AMP goes back to the stock page. A page key (SRC, FLTR, AMP, FX, MOD) leaves for that page.
  6. PLAY, STOP, RECORD, the trig keys and TRK + trig keep working while EUC+ is open. The pages follow the active track.

Every track has its own EUC+ settings. After STOP, PLAY starts the riff from its first bar. A pause (PLAY while playing) and PLAY again carry on where it was.

Two pages are in v0.2. Two more are planned. The planned pages are a design, and their names and ranges may change before they are built.

Known limits in v0.2

  • Settings live in memory and reset at power-on. Saving them is v0.3.
  • Steps with any parameter lock keep their stock note.
  • Digitakt II OS 1.17 only. Another OS version means finding every address again.
  • Not yet run on a real unit. Audio-track repitching has not been checked by ear yet.

Installing

There is no ready-made download. The built OS file contains Elektron's firmware, so it cannot be shared. You build it yourself, on your own computer, from the stock OS file Elektron publishes, with the Elekloader tools. Source and build steps are in the project's repository.

Before you flash: rehearse recovery
  1. Back up your projects, sounds and samples with Elektron Transfer.
  2. Connect a known-good 5-pin DIN MIDI interface to the Digitakt's MIDI IN. USB is not heard by the recovery menu, and some cheap USB-MIDI cables send nothing at all.
  3. Hold FUNC while powering on, press TRIG 4 (never TRIG 2 or 3: they erase data), and send the stock OS 1.17 file from Transfer over DIN. Do this once with the stock file before you ever need it.
  4. Never power off during an update. Afterwards, check the version the unit shows.

Please do not post or ask about firmware mods on Elektronauts: Elektron's policy since 16 September 2026 does not allow it.
